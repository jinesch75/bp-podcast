What is counted — and what is not.
  • Only numbers: each site visit, episode opened, listen started or listened to the end adds 1 to that day's counter, per episode and language.
  • Nothing about the listener is received or stored: no IP address, no cookie, no device or browser, no time of day, no registration. Listeners cannot be told apart, so these are numbers of listens, not of people.
  • A listen is counted once per episode and language each time the page is loaded; reloading the page counts again. Partner review pages are not counted.
