Lëtz prepare – Ready for 72 Hours

Would you be ready if the power went off for three days? Anna and Tom present Lëtz prepare, Luxembourg's National Resilience Strategy, and the guide to 72 hours of self-sufficiency: warnings, a checklist for your home, and a personal emergency plan.
